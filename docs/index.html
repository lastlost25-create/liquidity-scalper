⚠ Discipline rules

Advice, not strategy — listen before you trade.
1Never compromise on the rules. No exceptions, no "just this once".
2Every trade gets a stop loss — a hard stop in the system, always. Never a mental stop.
3When the market proves you wrong, accept it. Step away, find the next trade later. Don't fight the market — it will take you out.
4Wait for the last level. Take only the BEST trade — any strategy works if you only take the best.
5Don't chase daily profit — that ruins you one day. Think in months: losing days are fine, the month is what counts.
